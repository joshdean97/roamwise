Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This notice explains how LeavePrints uses personal data when you visit the site, create an account, plan a trip or share a public Print.

1. Who we are

LeavePrints is operated by Josh Dean trading as LeavePrints. For UK data protection law, Josh Dean is the controller of the personal data described in this notice.

Email: hello@leaveprints.com

2. What personal data we collect

Account data

When you register, we collect your username and email address. We store a secure hash of your password rather than the password itself. We also record whether your email has been confirmed and, for new accounts, when and under which version of the Terms you agreed to use LeavePrints.

Trip and planner data

We store the trips you choose to save, which can include route stops, nights, dates, travel style, display currency, estimated costs, transport choices, transport costs and transport notes.

Public sharing data

If you explicitly make a trip public, LeavePrints creates an opaque sharing link so other people can view that Print. Public sharing is off by default.

Technical and security data

When you use LeavePrints, technical data such as IP address, request details, timestamps, browser information and security events may be processed by LeavePrints and its hosting providers. We use this to operate the service, prevent abuse, investigate errors and protect accounts.

First-party product analytics

LeavePrints records a small set of product events in its own database so we can understand whether the beta is useful — for example, opening the planner, adding the first or second city, saving a trip, enabling a public Print or loading a shared route. Authenticated events can be associated with your internal user id. Anonymous landing-page and public-Print views are counted without a persistent visitor identifier. The analytics event store does not record your email, username, IP address, browser fingerprint or free-form trip notes.

Emails and support

We process your email address to send transactional messages such as email confirmation and password-reset links. If you contact us, we also process the information you include in that message.

4. Who we share personal data with

We use a small number of service providers to run LeavePrints. They may process personal data only as needed to provide their services to us.

  • Render — application and database hosting, networking and operational logs.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery, including confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • Bunny Fonts — the landing page currently loads web fonts from Bunny's font service, which can receive technical request data such as your IP address.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a legitimate business reorganisation. If the providers we use change materially, we will update this notice.

5. Public Prints

A trip is private unless you choose to make it public. Once enabled, anyone who obtains the public link may be able to view the trip information included on that public page and copy the route into their own planner.

Do not put sensitive or identifying information into content that you intend to share publicly. You can revoke a public share from LeavePrints, but we cannot control copies, screenshots or information already saved by someone else.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

LeavePrints currently uses first-party session and security storage needed for functions such as login state, form security and delivering the service you ask for. We do not currently use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies.

Product analytics is recorded server-side in the LeavePrints database and does not add an analytics cookie, browser-storage identifier or third-party analytics script. Because the current browser storage remains limited to essential service/security functions, LeavePrints does not currently show a cookie-consent banner. If we later add non-essential tracking technologies, we will update this notice and introduce any consent controls required at that time.

7. How long we keep personal data

Account and saved-trip data is generally kept while your account remains active because it is needed to provide LeavePrints. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove personal data from the active service when it is no longer needed, subject to information we must retain for legal, security or dispute-resolution reasons.

Limited copies may remain temporarily in routine backups, email-delivery records or operational logs until those systems rotate or expire them. Security and diagnostic information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the security and reliability purpose for which it was collected.

Until self-service account deletion is available, you can request deletion at hello@leaveprints.com.

8. International transfers

Some providers that support LeavePrints may process data in countries outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for a transfer, we rely on legally recognised mechanisms used by the relevant provider, such as adequacy regulations or approved contractual safeguards.

9. Security

We use measures intended to protect personal data, including HTTPS, password hashing, email confirmation, access controls, CSRF protection, rate limiting and security headers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong, unique password and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

10. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law can give you rights to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is used;
  • access a copy of your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask for erasure of personal data;
  • restrict certain processing;
  • object to certain processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive certain data in a portable format; and
  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

To exercise a right, email hello@leaveprints.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Children

LeavePrints is designed for independent travellers and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to LeavePrints inappropriately, please contact us so we can review it.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Policy as LeavePrints changes or legal requirements develop. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be communicated in a reasonable way where appropriate.
